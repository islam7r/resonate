Resonate

The advanced, privacy-first offline player.

Your music lives on your device. No account, no streaming, nothing watching what you listen to. Just an unusually capable player for files you already own.

What it does

Built for people who keep their own music

Bootlegs, live sets, DJ mixes, classical box sets, lossless rips — the collections that every mainstream player organises badly.

Free

Folder-first browsing

Navigates the directories actually on disk, not an imposed Artist ▸ Album hierarchy. Metadata sorting is an option inside a folder, not a cage around it.

Free

Every format that matters

FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC. Lossless files are badged as such, because the difference is the point.

Free

The Precision Wave scrubber

The transport is the waveform — geometric bars drawn from the track's own amplitude envelope, breathing with the low end as it plays.

Pro

Stem separation, on device

Split any file into vocals, drums, bass and other in seconds. Nothing uploaded, works with the network off. Isolate a part mid-song without a gap.

Pro

Haptic bass

The Taptic Engine follows the 20–160 Hz band in real time. A sustained layer for the bassline, a transient hit for the kick.

Pro

A theme engine that means it

Not a colour swap. A platter turning at 33⅓ with a tonearm that tracks the song, or a cassette whose reels spool in real time.

Discover

“Do I already own this?”

Point it at whatever is playing in the room. Resonate recognises the song acoustically against a catalogue built from your own files — offline, on device — and plays your copy. Your rip, your bitrate, no network.

If you don't own it, it goes to a Discovery Log with where and when you heard it, and a link to buy it. Copyrighted audio is never downloaded.

Privacy, concretely

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics
  • Library, play counts and discovery log never leave the device
  • Stem separation and song matching run locally
  • Network used only to name unowned songs, fetch artwork, check for updates and serve ads in the free tier
  • Location is opt-in and off by default
